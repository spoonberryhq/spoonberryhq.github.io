Spoonberry

Recipes, meal plans and groceries for the whole household.

Save recipes from any website, a cookbook page or a friend's message. Plan the week, and the grocery list fills itself in, sorted by aisle. Everyone at home shares the same recipes, plan and list through iCloud, and Cook Mode keeps the screen on with timers that ring even when the phone is on silent.

Made for iPhone Duo

Spoonberry is built for iPhone Duo as well as every other iPhone. Unfold it and the recipe list sits beside the recipe, with the ingredients beside the steps. Stand it half-folded on the counter and Cook Mode splits at the fold: the step on one half, big controls and timers on the other. Fold it shut and carry on where you left off. On iPad you get the same side-by-side layout.

For iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad. No account, no ads, no tracking.
