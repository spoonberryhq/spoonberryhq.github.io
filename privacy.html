Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Spoonberry is a recipe app for you and your household. We don't collect, see or store your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your recipes are kept

Your recipes, photos, meal plans, grocery lists and pantry are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your own iCloud account so they stay in step across your devices. Apple runs iCloud; we can't read anything in it. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles iCloud data.

Sharing with your household

When you invite someone to your household, Spoonberry uses iCloud sharing to give them your recipes, meal plan, grocery lists and pantry. Everyone in the household can see and change them, and sees the name from each person's iCloud account next to what they added. You can remove people, or leave a household, at any time from the Household screen.

Saving recipes from the web

When you save a recipe from a web page, Spoonberry downloads that page straight from the website, the same way Safari would, and keeps the recipe and its photo. Those websites may have their own privacy policies. Spoonberry only contacts the pages you choose to save.

Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Spoonberry uses Apple's on-device model to read recipes from scans and pasted text. This happens on your device; the text isn't sent to us or anyone else.

Camera, photos and Siri

Spoonberry uses the camera and your photo library only when you take or pick a photo for a recipe or scan a cookbook page. Siri and Shortcuts can add items to your grocery list and open recipes; Apple processes those requests under its own privacy policy.

Backups

A backup you export is a file you choose where to keep. It holds your recipes, photos, meal plan, grocery lists and pantry, and stays wherever you save it.

Children

Spoonberry doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
