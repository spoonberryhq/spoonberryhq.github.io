Common questions

How do I share recipes with my household?

Open Household, tap Invite to Household, and send the invitation by Messages or email. When they open it on their iPhone or iPad, your recipes, meal plan, grocery lists and pantry appear in their Spoonberry. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. If you use Family Sharing, the people in your family can download Spoonberry without buying it again.

How do I save a recipe from a website?

On the recipe's page in Safari or another app, tap the Share button and choose Spoonberry. The recipe waits in your Inbox to be checked and saved. Or tap + in Recipes and choose Import from Web.

Can I bring my recipes over from Paprika?

Yes. In Paprika, export your recipes, then open the .paprikarecipes file with Spoonberry from Files, Mail or AirDrop, or tap + in Recipes and choose Import from Paprika. Photos, ratings and categories come across too.

My changes aren't showing up on another phone.

Check that both phones are signed in to iCloud and online. Open Household to see whether syncing is paused. Changes usually arrive within a few seconds.

How do I back up my recipes?

Open Household and tap Export Backup. Keep the file somewhere other than iCloud, like a Mac. To bring it back, tap Restore from Backup on the same screen and pick the file.
